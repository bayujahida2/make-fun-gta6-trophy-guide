Roadmap to the platinum

Six phases, in order. One playthrough is enough: there are no online trophies and only one trophy can be missed. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter; the game has 15 slots plus one autosave, and no mission replay.

Sources and notes

Official trophy names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles. Guides are written in our own words for this entry and cite their sources by name beside each trophy: PlayStation, Exophase, PSNProfiles, PowerPyx, GTABase, Output Lag, EGamers, GTAVibes, KeenGamer, OfZenAndComputing and GTAViceCityHub. Launch-era details (the Chapter 1 meditation, the Yago race, the unconfirmed triggers) come from Output Lag, PowerPyx and GTABase, checked against each other where they agree.

Cheats are reported to switch off trophies and saving, so keep them off your trophy save. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or screenshot is used anywhere in this guide; the look is our own.
